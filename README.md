# GST Complete Course Handbook (Modules 1 - 4)

Comprehensive Goods and Services Tax (GST) web handbook and practical compliance guide, compiled from G-TEC Nergy Vidya certification materials.

## Structure
- **Module 1**: Introduction of GST in India & Concepts/Application (Chapters 1 & 2)
- **Module 2**: GST Registration (Chapter 3)
- **Module 3**: Tax Invoices, Credit/Debit Notes & E-Way Bills (Chapters 4 & 5)
- **Module 4**: E-Filing of GSTR-1, GSTR-2A/2B, GSTR-3B, CMP-08 & Payment of Tax (Chapters 6 to 10)

## Features
- Fully responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- Instant full-text search across all 10 chapters
- Dark / Light mode toggle with local storage persistence
- Embedded compliance forms, portal screenshots, and rate tables
- Pure static zero-dependency web app ready for edge CDN hosting on Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages

## Deployment
Published on Cloudflare Pages and GitHub.
